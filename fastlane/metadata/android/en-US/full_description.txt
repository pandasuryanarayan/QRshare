An all-in-one, lightweight QR tool: generate codes and read them, with no ads, no
tracking and no Play Services.

<ul>
<li>Share a link, contact or text from any app and get a QR code instantly.</li>
<li>Scan with the camera, or decode a QR code straight out of a screenshot.</li>
<li>Templates for Wi-Fi, contacts (vCard), UPI payments, calendar events, locations,
email, SMS, phone numbers and 2FA secrets.</li>
<li>Choose the error-correction level, or let the app pick the strongest one that fits.</li>
<li>Small files can travel inside a single code, compressed and recoverable.</li>
<li>Save to the gallery, copy the image, or share it anywhere.</li>
<li>History of every code you generated or scanned.</li>
<li>Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcuts for one-tap scanning.</li>
</ul>
